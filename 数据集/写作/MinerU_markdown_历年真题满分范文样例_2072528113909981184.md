【分类：四级范文｜年份：2024年12月｜题目：The Importance of Developing Good Habits】

It is universally acknowledged that good habits exert a profound influence on people’s whole life. Forming positive daily habits helps us manage time reasonably and improve self-discipline.

When we stick to reading every day, we can continuously enrich our knowledge reserve and broaden our horizons. By contrast, staying up late and procrastination will hinder personal progress in the long run.

【分类：四级范文｜年份：2024年6月｜题目：Whether University Library Should Be Open to Public】

In the modern era, there is a heated debate regarding whether the university's library should be open to the public. Some believe that it will do us more good than harm, while others hold opposite opinions.

Opening libraries to the public provides ordinary citizens with free access to reading resources and promotes social reading atmosphere. However, too many outsiders may occupy seats and disturb normal study of college students.

In my opinion, universities can set fixed visiting hours and manage entry rules to balance public demand and students’ interests.

【分类：四级范文｜年份：2022年12月｜题目：The Importance of Reading】

Nowadays, more young people tend to scroll short videos rather than read books quietly. However, reading still plays an irreplaceable role in personal growth.

Reading classic books helps us absorb knowledge, shape our values and improve expression ability. Besides, deep reading calms our mind from noisy social media information.

Therefore, we should arrange fixed reading time every week and develop a lifelong reading habit.

【分类：六级范文｜年份：2024年12月｜题目：The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Daily Life】

Nowadays, artificial intelligence has profoundly changed people’s work and lifestyle in various fields. It greatly improves work efficiency and simplifies repetitive daily tasks for ordinary people.

However, over-dependence on AI may weaken people’s independent thinking and problem-solving abilities. Some people tend to rely totally on AI to finish study assignments without independent consideration.

From my perspective, we should make rational use of artificial intelligence as a helpful tool rather than a complete substitute for personal thinking.

【分类：六级范文｜年份：2022年6月｜题目：Online Learning’s Advantages and Disadvantages】

Online learning has become increasingly popular after years of popularization. It breaks time and space restrictions, enabling learners to take courses anytime and anywhere.

Nevertheless, online study requires strong self-discipline. Many students lack supervision and waste time playing games during class, leading to poor learning effects.

As far as I am concerned, we should combine online resources and offline classroom learning for better study outcomes.

As an old saying goes, "Success belongs to the persevering." This proverb tells us that persistence is the key to achieving long-term goals.
