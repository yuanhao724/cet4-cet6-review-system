一、四级翻译真题（2016.12–2025.12）

2016.12 红色文化（传统文化）

中文原文：

在中国文化中，红色通常象征着好运、长寿和幸福。在春节和其他喜庆场合，红色到处可见。人们把现金作为礼物送给家人或亲密朋友时，通常放在红信封里。红色在中国流行的另一个原因是人们把它与中国革命相联系。然而，红色并不总是代表好运与快乐。从前死者的名字常用红色书写，用红墨水写中国人名被看作一种冒犯。

参考译文：

In Chinese culture, red usually symbolizes good luck, longevity and happiness. Red can be seen everywhere during the Spring Festival and other festive occasions. When people give cash as gifts to family members or close friends, they usually put the money in red envelopes. Another reason for red’s popularity is that people associate it with China’s revolution. However, red does not always stand for good fortune and joy. In ancient times, the names of the deceased were often written in red, so writing Chinese people’s names in red ink is regarded as an offense.

2019.6 灯笼（传统文化）

中文原文：

灯笼起源于东汉，最初主要用于照明。在唐代，人们用灯笼庆祝安定富足的生活，从那时起，灯笼在中国各地流行开来。灯笼一般用色彩鲜艳的薄纸制作，形状、尺寸各不相同。在中国传统文化里，红灯笼象征生活美满、生意兴旺，常在春节、元宵节悬挂。如今全球很多国家都能见到红灯笼。

参考译文：

The lantern dates back to the Eastern Han Dynasty and was originally used for lighting. During the Tang Dynasty, people hung lanterns to celebrate a peaceful and prosperous life, and lanterns have been popular across China ever since. Made of thin bright-colored paper, lanterns come in various shapes and sizes. In traditional Chinese culture, red lanterns symbolize happy family life and booming business, and they are widely hung during the Spring Festival and Lantern Festival. Today, red lanterns can be seen in many countries around the world.

2021.6 教育公平（教育话题）

中文原文：

为促进教育公平，中国已投入 360 亿元改善农村教育设施，强化中西部农村义务教育。这笔资金用于修缮校舍、购置图书，惠及 16 万余所中小学；同时采购音乐、美术教学器材。如今山区、农村孩子和沿海城市学生一样，可以正常上艺术课程。不少曾经为了优质教育前往城市就读的学生，现在选择回到本地乡村学校。

参考译文：

To promote educational equality, China has invested 36 billion yuan to upgrade rural educational facilities and boost compulsory education in central and western rural areas. The funds are used to renovate school buildings and purchase books, benefiting more than 160,000 primary and secondary schools. Money is also spent on musical and painting equipment. Nowadays, children in mountainous and rural areas can take art classes just like students in coastal cities. Many students who once moved to cities for better education now choose to return to local rural schools.

2022.6 中医（传统文化 / 民生）

中文原文：

中医是中国传统医学的重要分支，拥有数千年历史。中医依靠草药、针灸、推拿调节人体机能，主张人与自然和谐共生。中医诊疗温和、副作用小，深受普通百姓喜爱。近些年中医走出国门，在全球多国开设诊所，被列入世界非物质文化遗产，获得国际广泛认可。

参考译文：

Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) is a vital branch of China’s traditional medicine with a history of thousands of years. It regulates human bodily functions through herbal medicine, acupuncture and massage, advocating harmony between human beings and nature. Gentle in treatment and with minor side effects, TCM is deeply popular among ordinary people. In recent years, TCM has gone global, with clinics opened in many countries. It has been inscribed on the World Intangible Cultural Heritage List and gained wide international recognition.

2023.12 中国饮食（社会 / 民生）

中文原文：

改革开放以来，中国人的饮食发生巨大变化。过去经济落后，食物种类单一、供给不足，人们只求吃饱。如今经济飞速发展，食材丰富多样，食品品质大幅提升。伴随生活水平提高，大众越来越注重饮食营养，低脂、低糖、有机健康食品在市场广受欢迎。

参考译文：

Since the reform and opening-up, Chinese people’s diet has witnessed dramatic changes. In the past, due to backward economy, food varieties were limited and in short supply, so people only aimed to get enough to eat. Today, with rapid economic growth, food ingredients are abundant and their quality has greatly improved. As living standards rise, people pay increasing attention to nutritional balance, making low-fat, low-sugar and organic healthy foods widely popular in the market.

2024.12 移动支付（科技 / 社会）

中文原文：

移动支付在中国普及度极高，人们出门无需携带现金、银行卡。超市购物、街边小吃、公交地铁，全部可以扫码完成付款。移动支付简化交易流程，带动电商、直播行业发展，彻底改变国民消费习惯。如今东南亚、欧美多国纷纷引进这套便捷支付模式。

参考译文：

Mobile payment is extremely prevalent in China, allowing people to go out without cash or bank cards. People can scan QR codes to pay for supermarket goods, street food, buses and subways. Mobile payment simplifies transaction procedures, fuels the growth of e-commerce and live-streaming industries, and completely transforms Chinese consumers’ spending habits. Nowadays, many countries in Southeast Asia, Europe and America have introduced this convenient payment system.

2025.6 剪纸（传统文化）

中文原文：

剪纸是中国传统民间艺术，拥有两千多年历史。春节、婚礼等喜庆场合，人们都会张贴剪纸，寓意吉祥顺遂。剪纸题材涵盖花鸟、人物、历史传说，寄托普通人对美好生活的向往。剪纸现已列入国家级非物质文化遗产，常年吸引海内外游客专程观赏、学习。

参考译文：

Paper-cutting is a traditional Chinese folk art with a history of more than 2,000 years. During festive occasions such as the Spring Festival and weddings, people paste paper-cuts to symbolize good luck and smooth lives. Covering flowers, birds, human figures and historical legends, paper-cutting carries ordinary people’s longing for a better life. Paper-cutting has been listed as national Intangible Cultural Heritage, attracting tourists from home and abroad to watch and learn every year.

2025.12 民营经济（经济话题）

中文原文：

近年来，中国政府高度重视民营经济发展，出台大量扶持政策。截至 2025 年 3 月，全国民营企业总量突破 5700 万家，占全部市场主体 92.3%。民营企业数量稳步增长，产业结构持续优化，研发投入逐年上涨，在人工智能、数字信息技术领域发展迅猛。民营经济的创新活力，为国内经济高质量发展提供坚实支撑。

参考译文：

In recent years, the Chinese government has attached great importance to the development of private economy and issued a large number of supportive policies. By the end of March 2025, the number of private enterprises nationwide exceeded 57 million, accounting for 92.3% of all market entities. Private enterprises have seen steady growth in quantity and continuous optimization of industrial structure, with rising annual investment in research and development. They develop rapidly in artificial intelligence and digital information technology. The innovative vitality of private economy offers solid support for high-quality domestic economic growth.

2024.6 碳中和（生态环保）

中文原文：

碳中和是全球近年热议的环保发展目标。中国大力发展风电、光伏等清洁能源，推行垃圾分类、低碳出行全民政策。工业减排、国土植树造林同步推进，平衡二氧化碳总排放量。实现碳中和无法一蹴而就，需要政府、企业、全体民众长期协同努力。

参考译文：

Carbon neutrality is a hotly discussed environmental development goal worldwide in recent years. China vigorously develops clean energy such as wind power and photovoltaic power, and carries out nationwide policies including garbage classification and low-carbon travel. Industrial emission reduction and national afforestation are advanced simultaneously to balance total carbon dioxide emissions. Carbon neutrality cannot be achieved overnight; it requires long-term joint efforts from governments, enterprises and all citizens.

2025.6 丝绸之路（传统文化 / 经济）

中文原文：

丝绸之路始于汉代，是古代连通亚欧大陆的商贸通道。古代商人向西运送丝绸、瓷器，带回香料、珠宝与异域文化。丝绸之路不仅促进商品流通，更推动东西方思想、艺术、民俗深度交流，深刻影响世界文明进程。如今 “一带一路” 延续古代丝路开放包容、互利共赢的核心精神。

参考译文：

Originating in the Han Dynasty, the Silk Road was an ancient trade route connecting Asia and Europe. Ancient merchants transported silk and porcelain to the West, and brought back spices, jewelry and exotic cultures. The Silk Road not only boosted commodity circulation, but also promoted in-depth exchanges of ideology, art and folk customs between the East and the West, profoundly shaping the course of world civilization. Today, the Belt and Road Initiative carries forward the ancient Silk Road’s core spirit of openness, inclusiveness and mutual benefit.

2025.6 天宫空间站（科技）

中文原文：

天宫空间站是中国自主建造的首个长期在轨太空实验室，内部可用空间超 110 立方米，可长期容纳三名航天员，轨道距地表 400 至 450 公里。空间站已落地 180 余项科研项目，覆盖空间生命科学、太空医学、新型航天材料等领域。太空育种等衍生技术落地产业，创造数千亿经济价值。天宫代表中国航天重大突破，也为全球太空探索贡献中国力量。

参考译文：

The Tiangong Space Station is China’s first self-built long-term orbital space laboratory, with over 110 cubic meters of usable internal space that can permanently accommodate three astronauts, orbiting 400 to 450 kilometers above the earth’s surface. More than 180 scientific research projects have been carried out on the station, covering space life science, space medicine, new aerospace materials and other fields. Derivative technologies such as space breeding have been industrialized, generating hundreds of billions of economic value. Tiangong marks a major breakthrough in China’s aerospace industry and contributes Chinese strength to global space exploration.

2025.12 尊老美德（社会发展 / 文化）

中文原文：

尊老是中华民族延续千年的传统美德，根植于国人日常行为与价值观念，是全社会通用的行为准则。当代中国持续传承这一美德，全社会共同营造敬老、助老氛围。社区开设老年食堂，公共场所设置老人优先座位，政府出台法规全方位保障老年人合法权益。尊老能够维护社会和谐，持续推动全社会文明进步。

参考译文：

Respecting the elderly is a thousand-year-old traditional virtue of the Chinese nation, rooted in people’s daily behaviors and values, and serves as a universal code of conduct for the whole society. Contemporary China continues to inherit this virtue, and all sectors of society jointly build an atmosphere of respecting and assisting the elderly. Communities have opened canteens for seniors, priority seats for the elderly are arranged in public places, and the government has issued laws and regulations to fully protect the legitimate rights and interests of senior citizens. Respecting the elderly safeguards social harmony and continuously boosts the civilized progress of the whole society.

2026.6 无人机产业（科技 / 经济）

中文原文：

近十年中国无人机产业飞速崛起，整机出口总量常年全球第一。国产无人机凭借稳定性能、高清拍摄、简易操作，广受海外消费者欢迎，广泛应用于各类民用场景。作为国家战略性新兴产业，无人机逐步转型多功能智能设备，覆盖农业、物流、测绘多个领域。未来无人机将拓展更多应用场景，持续为社会经济发展注入新动能。

参考译文：

Over the past decade, China’s drone industry has risen rapidly, with its total export volume of complete drones ranking first worldwide all year round. With stable performance, high-definition shooting and simple operation, domestic drones are well received by overseas consumers and widely applied to all kinds of civil scenarios. As a national strategic emerging industry, drones are gradually transformed into multi-functional intelligent devices, covering agriculture, logistics, mapping and many other fields. In the future, drones will expand more application scenarios and continuously inject new momentum into social and economic development.

2022.12 故宫（传统文化）

中文原文：

故宫坐落于北京城市中心，始建于明代，曾是明清两代皇家宫殿。馆内收藏百万件珍贵文物，涵盖书法、古画、瓷器、玉器，完整还原古代宫廷生活与传统审美。近些年故宫上线线上数字展厅，免费向全球开放，吸引大量青年群体主动了解中国古代历史文化。

参考译文：

Located in the center of Beijing, the Palace Museum was built in the Ming Dynasty and served as the imperial palace for the Ming and Qing dynasties. It collects millions of precious cultural relics including calligraphy, ancient paintings, porcelain and jade, fully restoring ancient royal life and traditional aesthetics. In recent years, the Palace Museum has launched online digital exhibition halls open to the whole world for free, attracting a large number of young people to take the initiative to learn about ancient Chinese history and culture.

2023.6 人工智能（科技 / 社会）

中文原文：

近十年人工智能技术高速发展，广泛落地医疗、教育、工业、交通等行业。人工智能大幅提升生产效率，简化日常办事流程，但同时改变传统就业结构。世界各国都在出台规范政策，平衡智能技术便利与潜在社会风险，引导人工智能健康、可持续发展。

参考译文：

Artificial intelligence technology has developed at high speed over the past decade and has been widely applied in industries including medical care, education, industry and transportation. AI greatly improves production efficiency and simplifies daily procedures, yet it transforms the traditional employment structure at the same time. Countries around the world are issuing regulatory policies to balance the convenience of intelligent technology and potential social risks, guiding the healthy and sustainable development of artificial intelligence.
