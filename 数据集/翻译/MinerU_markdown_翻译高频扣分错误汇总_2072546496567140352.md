1. 专有名词直译错误（大量丢分）

错误：paper cut（剪纸）｜正确：paper-cutting

错误：spring festival（节日首字母必须大写）｜正确：the Spring Festival

错误：no material culture heritage｜正确：Intangible Cultural Heritage

错误：China people｜正确：Chinese people / People in China

错误：emperor king（皇帝）｜正确：emperor

2. 词汇搭配不地道（阅卷直接扣 1–2 分 / 处）

错误：develop culture｜正确：inherit /promote culture

错误：do pollution reduction｜正确：cut carbon emissions

错误：cheap price｜正确：low price（price 只能用 high/low 修饰）

错误：improve awareness｜正确：raise awareness

3. 语法基础错误（失分最多）

时态混乱：描述古代历史不用过去时

错误：The Great Wall is built in Qin Dynasty

正确：The Great Wall was built in the Qin Dynasty

冠词缺失：专有名词缺 the

错误：Silk Road｜正确：the Silk Road

主谓不一致

错误：The art have a long history

正确：The art has a long history

名词无单复数

错误：Teacher should protect student

正确：Teachers should protect students

4. 中式语序、逐字翻译（直接降档）

错误：I very like Chinese tea

正确：I like Chinese tea very much

错误：open the mobile payment（打开支付）

正确：use mobile payment

5. 语义遗漏扣分

原文数字、朝代、核心名词漏译直接降档，例：

原文 “故宫建于明朝”

错误译文：The Palace Museum was built.（漏 the Ming Dynasty，直接少 2 分）
